Academics & Research

I'm a senior undergraduate at the University of British Columbia, pursuing Combined Honours in Physics and Mathematics. My research is in experimental quantum physics — specifically, the places where quantum matter does something that shouldn't be possible and then turns out to be useful.

I'm drawn to systems where the ground state itself is the interesting object: frustrated magnets, correlated electrons, exotic quasiparticles. The weirder the physics, the more carefully I want to understand it.

Current — Rogge Group, UNSW (2024–Present)

Quantum Spin Liquids in Si:P

Primary project of a year-long research practicum. Using scanning tunnelling microscopy to investigate quantum spin liquid behaviour in phosphorus-doped silicon — a system that sits at the edge of the Mott transition and hosts highly frustrated magnetic interactions. STM gives us atomically resolved access to a regime most probes can't touch.

Strongly Correlated Systems STM Quantum Magnetism Si:P
Current — Rogge Group, UNSW

Superconductivity in Si:P via Low-Temperature STM

A self-initiated project extending the group's Si:P programme. Motivated by connections to my earlier work on superconducting resonators, I'm exploring signatures of superconductivity in this platform using low-temperature STM — probing how correlated electron physics and superconducting order coexist in a system with atomic-scale control.

Superconductivity Low-Temperature STM Condensed Matter
Past — Salfi Lab, UBC (2024)

3D Flip-Chip Architecture for Hole-Spin Qubit Readout

Developed and fabricated a flip-chip architecture to enable fast dispersive readout of hole-spin qubits via superconducting resonators. Hands-on nanofabrication throughout: reactive ion etching, maskless alignment, electron beam evaporation. Characterised resonators on SOI using a vector network analyser.

Spin Qubits Nanofabrication Superconducting Resonators SOI
Past — Salfi Lab, UBC (2023)

Transmon Qubit Architecture for a Cryogenic Photonic Interconnect

Designed and performed calculations for a superconducting transmon qubit architecture intended to benchmark a cryogenic photonic interconnect at UBC. Grounded me in the fundamentals of circuit QED and superconducting device design.

Transmon Qubits Circuit QED Cryogenic Systems
Past — Math Summer Reading Programme, UBC (2023)

Wagstaff's Theorem on Arithmetic Progressions

Authored an expository paper on Samuel Wagstaff's 1972 result bounding sets that avoid arithmetic progressions. Explored measure-theoretic tools — Hausdorff dimension, density, and length — and their implications for additive combinatorics.

Personal Operating System

I'm a literal nihilist. Nothing has inherent meaning — which means meaning has to be built deliberately, or not at all. I chose to build it.

Over the past year I've developed a personal optimization system I call the Number: a daily score that quantifies how well I'm living according to my own stated values. It runs on 14-day iterations, each with a calibration document that defines the rules exactly. When the iteration ends, I rewrite the calibration. The system evolves. The score persists.

The point isn't the number. The point is that the number makes akrasia visible — and visible things can be reasoned about.

N = H + E − D + C

The Number

Four components scored daily. Health — gym, nutrition, routine. Enrichment — anything that feeds the mind: music, reading, scuba, cricket. Career — research, study, lab work, weighted by depth of engagement. Degeneracy — a penalty term. Banned behaviours, sleep quality, failures of self-regulation. Target: ~100 per day.

Dual Mode

Designer & Player

The Player executes. The Designer plans, scores, and calibrates — a dispassionate observer of the Player's day. Scoring happens in Designer mode: the Player's failures are not the Designer's failures. The separation matters. It's what makes honest self-assessment possible without spiralling.

14-Day Cycle

Iteration & Deload

Each 14-day iteration has a calibration document: exact rules, point values, permitted and banned behaviours. Saturday is deload — no plan, no career score, act on wishes. The degeneracy rules still apply. At the end of each iteration the Designer rewrites the calibration from scratch, carrying forward what worked and discarding what didn't.
